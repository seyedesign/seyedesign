Founder · WeFrame Design community Since 2024

Bringing curious minds together.

How teaching Figma to a few friends grew into WeFrame: a community where designers, developers and others learn, meet and practise on real products together.

My role

Founder and community lead

The community

Mostly designers, plus developers and people from other fields

Started

Early 2024, as informal online sessions

01

It started with a favour.

Two friends, Ali and Milad, wanted to learn Figma and asked if I could help. I said yes, then asked who else wanted to join.

Every Friday morning we met online. No formal course, no big plan: just people who wanted to learn together.

02

We didn’t plan to build a community.

The Friday sessions were never meant to become anything bigger. But people kept coming back, then started bringing friends. We started by learning together. We stayed because we enjoyed growing together.

  1. 01Learning togetherA few Figma lessons on Friday mornings, for anyone who wanted to join.
  2. 02Finding our peopleThe same faces kept showing up, and conversations carried on after the sessions.
  3. 03Growing togetherWe gave it a name, WeFrame, and started organising more on purpose.

As the group grew, so did what we did together: more sessions, values we agreed on, and later meetups and design challenges.

03

From lessons to real work.

Many of our members were early in their careers and wanted real professional experience. Practice screens only go so far, so we started design challenges on real products.

The first was a redesign of the 30book (سی‌بوک) website. It got good feedback, so for the next one we looked for a sponsor with a real brief.

  1. 01A real briefA business shares a real page and its goals
  2. 02Participants’ designsMembers redesign it on their own
  3. 03Professional feedbackAn experienced designer judges the work
  4. 04A community eventResults, talks and a chance to meet

For the second challenge, Soheil Abbasi, senior innovation manager at Hamava, supported us as sponsor. Twelve participants redesigned the mentors page of Khoshfekri, a mentoring website, focusing on understanding the business, UX principles and solutions they could defend.

The feedback · judged by Alireza Haji, senior product designer at Tadbir Pardaz
Product designStandard, defensible design; a fresh point of view; UI basics; a clear presentation
BusinessUnderstanding the product; staying true to the brief
PeoplePassion, experience and fit for working together
The event · Shahrivar 1403 (Aug–Sep 2024)

Results night at Zaviye.

We announced the results at Zaviye, with Soheil Abbasi. Along the way, participants worked through wireframing, the MVP process and designing for real needs.

The winnerA job as Khoshfekri’s website designer, plus a 3 million toman cash prize.
